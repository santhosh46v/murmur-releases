## Install

### macOS
1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **MurMur** into **Applications**.
2. First time only: right-click **MurMur** in Applications, choose **Open**, then confirm. (macOS shows "Apple cannot check it" because the app is not signed yet.)
3. Allow **Microphone** and **Screen Recording** when asked, so MurMur can hear the call and read your screen.

### Windows
1. Run the `.exe` installer.
2. First time only: if you see **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What MurMur does

- **Hears both sides** of the call, labelled *Them* and *You*, in 10 languages.
- **Answers you can say out loud**, in seconds, with the key phrases highlighted. Make any answer *Concise* or *Elaborate* with one click.
- **Reads your screen** on a shortcut, so it can help with a coding problem, a slide or a document.
- **Notes and history.** Every meeting is saved to your private history, with notes and action items you can copy, and you can continue a meeting later.
